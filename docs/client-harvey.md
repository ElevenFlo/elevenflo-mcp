# Harvey

ElevenFlo is listed in
[Harvey's Connector Library](https://www.harvey.ai/platform/connector-library).
Harvey administrators choose which connectors are enabled for a workspace. If
ElevenFlo isn't available to you in Harvey, ask your administrator to enable it.

Once it is enabled:

1. Ask Harvey about a bankruptcy case.
2. When Harvey asks you to connect ElevenFlo, sign in with your own ElevenFlo
   account and approve access.
3. Follow [Verify the connection](verify.md) and check the cited filing.

Each person connects their own ElevenFlo account, so research credits and plan
features follow that account.
