# Harvey

ElevenFlo is in
[Harvey's Connector Library](https://www.harvey.ai/platform/connector-library).
You need a Harvey account and an ElevenFlo account.

1. A Harvey admin enables ElevenFlo for your workspace. If you don't see
   ElevenFlo in Harvey, ask your admin.
2. Ask Harvey about a bankruptcy case. When Harvey asks you to connect
   ElevenFlo, sign in and approve access.

Each person signs in with their own ElevenFlo account, so credits and plan
features follow that person.
